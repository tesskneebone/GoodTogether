GOODTOGETHER LA · OCTOBER 2026

NOTHIN' BUT SAND BEACH CLEANUP

Spend a Saturday morning on the sand with Heal the Bay and volunteers from
all over LA, clearing marine debris before it washes into the bay.

WHEN:  Saturday, October 17, 2026 · 10 AM – 12 PM
WHERE: Santa Monica Beach, Tower 20 (Bay Street)
COST:  Free, all are welcome

SIGN UP: https://support.healthebay.org/event/nothin-but-sand-october-2026/e846162/register/new/select-tickets

THE MORNING AT A GLANCE
10:00 AM  Check in, beach & safety talk, pick up supplies, start cleaning
11:45 AM  Raffle & prizes at the Hub Tent (Heal the Bay merch and more)
12:00 PM  Wrap up

WHY SHOW UP
- Pull harmful plastic and debris off an LA County beach before it reaches the ocean
- Collect and log data that feeds real scientific research
- Meet Heal the Bay's community partners and other volunteers who care about the coast
- Enter the on-the-sand raffle

BEFORE YOU GO
* Waiver required for every volunteer. Complete it before you arrive, using
  the same email you registered with (the waiver link is on the event page:
  https://support.healthebay.org/event/nothin-but-sand-october-2026/e846162).
  Have your confirmation email ready at check-in.
    - 18+ and families: complete the online waiver
    - Ages 14–17: a parent or guardian must sign
    - Under 14: a parent or guardian must sign AND stay with the child
* Bring your own gear: reusable gloves, a bucket or bag, water, sunscreen.
* Plan for parking: lots near Tower 20 fill up fast. Carpool, bike, walk,
  or leave extra time.
* Rain or shine: only called off for lightning or a beach closure. Follow
  @healthebay for day-of updates.

Bring a friend and come make a dent.
