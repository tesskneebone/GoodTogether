Why show up

  • Pull harmful plastic and debris off an LA County beach before it reaches the ocean
  • Collect and log data that feeds real scientific research
  • Meet Heal the Bay’s community partners and other volunteers who care about the coast
  • Enter the on-the-sand raffle

Before you go

Waiver required for every volunteer. Complete it before you arrive, using the same email you registered with (the waiver link is on the event page). Have your confirmation email ready at check-in.

18+ and families: complete the online waiver.
Ages 14–17: a parent or guardian must sign.
Under 14: a parent or guardian must sign and stay with the child during the cleanup.

🧤 Bring your own gear. Reusable gloves, a bucket or bag, a water bottle, and sunscreen.

🚗 Plan for parking. Beach lots near Tower 20 fill up fast and entry lines get long. Carpool, bike, walk, or leave extra time.

⛅ Rain or shine. The cleanup is only called off for lightning or a beach closure. Follow @healthebay for day-of updates.
